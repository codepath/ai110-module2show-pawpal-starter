Title: Warm Weather Pet Safety (AVMA)
URL: https://avma.org/resources/pet-owners/petcare/warm-weather-pet-safety
Species: dog

For dog exercise in warm weather, consult a veterinarian before starting a new program, especially for an overweight dog or a short-nosed breed. Bring water for both yourself and your pet.

In warm weather, plan walks outside the hottest part of the day. Take breaks, bring water, and ensure pets have access to fresh water and shade outdoors.
